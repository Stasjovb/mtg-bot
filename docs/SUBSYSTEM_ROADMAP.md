# Subsystem roadmap — which subsystem is the highest-leverage next lever

`docs/POOL_PARITY.md` reports the `subsystem-gated / Effect-uncovered`
BLOCKED tail as **one undifferentiated bucket of 169 cards**. That
single opaque number cannot answer the campaign's actual sequencing
question: *which subsystem, closed next, flips the most pool-parity
cards for the least scope?* This doc is the data-driven instrument for
that decision — the same way the round-57 residue re-walk made the
Effect-arm worklist honest.

Every one of the 169 `blocked_subsystem_gated` cards is attributed to
the subsystem(s) that gate it (a card may be **multi-gated**; all gates
are recorded). The leverage table ranks subsystems by tractability
then by *single-gate* leverage — a single-gated card flips the moment
its one subsystem closes, so single-gate count + single-gate
deck-count is the honest "cheap unlock" signal; a multi-gated card
needs *every* gate closed and so does not credit any one subsystem.

> **What "subsystem-gated" means here.** A card lands in this tier
> (precedence 4, reason 1 or 2 of `measure_pool_parity.py`) because it
> either (1) emits ≥1 Python `Effect` class with no covered Rust
> counterpart — the `Effect-uncovered` per-card long tail, NOT one
> subsystem — or (2) all its Effects ARE covered but the
> production-deck loader rejects the full card via a specific
> subsystem gate. The taxonomy below separates these honestly: the
> *named* subsystems (1) are the loader gates whose closure is a
> single defined substrate; `Effect-uncovered` / `Non-safe
> triggered ability` / `Unclassified` are explicitly flagged as
> **diffuse** — not a single closure, a per-card tail.

## Reading the table — the data-driven sequencing answer

**The single highest-leverage *tractable* next lever is the non-basic
land substrate** (recommended round-59), with cost-reduction a close
second:

1. **Non-basic land substrate — 23 cards, ALL 23 single-gated, 34,575
   cumulative decks, medium tractability.** This is the cheapest large
   unlock in the table. Every one of the 23 is gated by *this gate
   alone* (no multi-gate residue) — closing it flips all 23 pool-parity
   cards in one substrate slice. They are zero-Effect dual / shock /
   planet / cave / utility lands (Breeding Pool, Stomping Ground,
   Watery Grave, Godless Shrine, Sacred Foundry, the five "Memorial
   World" planet lands, …) blocked **only** because
   `_printing_is_bridge_basic_land` requires the `Basic` supertype.
   The CardDef bridge already round-trips the basic-land mana ability;
   extending the admit gate to non-basic lands whose only ability is a
   tapland/dual mana profile is a contained substrate slice. Top
   single-gate leverage: Secluded Starforge (7,570), Breeding Pool
   (4,712), Watery Grave (4,226), Stomping Ground (3,859).

### High-card-count subsystems that are NOT cheap unlocks (honesty)

- **Effect-uncovered (115 cards, 521,045 decks)** is the single
  largest bucket by both card count and deck-count — but it is
  **diffuse, not a subsystem**. Each of the 115 emits a bespoke /
  uncovered Python `Effect` class with no live Rust counterpart; this
  is the per-card *new-Rust-Effect long tail* (the residue worklist
  itemises only the 7 load-bearing `genuinely_missing` ones). There is
  no single closure here — it is exactly the X-from-count /
  one-arm-per-round substrate lane the campaign has been grinding for
  46 rounds. Its 31 single-gated members (Diplomatic Relations 29,818;
  Orbital Plunge 28,629; Depressurize 28,346 — high-deck instants/
  sorceries) are individually high-leverage but each is its own slice.

- **Carrier activated abilities (35 cards, 53,689 decks)** is large
  but **0 single-gated** — every one is also gated by another
  subsystem, AND the activated-ability substrate is a campaign-wide
  §2-decline (round-42: CardDef carries no activated-ability field; it
  would silently mistranslate). Closing it is sprawling AND flips zero
  cards on its own. Not a next lever.

- **Station / Spacecraft (9 cards, 54,095 decks)** is genuinely huge
  and only 1 single-gated (Galvanizing Sawship, 5,938 decks). The
  Warmaker Gunship / Extinguisher Battleship Effect-halves shipped
  rounds 32/52 yet the CARDS stay Station-gated — the Station
  charge-counter subsystem is the long-pole the round-46 longer-term
  picture flagged. **Honest call: high deck-count, but the single-gate
  yield (1 card) does not justify a sprawling subsystem ahead of the
  two clean medium unlocks.**

- **Non-safe triggered ability (54 cards, 194,640 decks)** is large
  but **0 single-gated** and **diffuse** — a long tail of distinct
  unbridged trigger events/shapes, each needing its own safe-trigger
  allowlist + bridge arm (the round-1→47 carddef-triggers lane). Not a
  single closure.

### Recommended sequencing

1. **Round-59: Non-basic land substrate** — 23 single-gated cards /
   34,575 decks flip in one medium slice; zero multi-gate residue
   means the yield is fully realized. Highest *clean* leverage.
2. **Round-60: Cost-reduction substrate** — 5 single-gated / 53,648
   decks (highest single-gate deck-count of the tractable named
   subsystems); same cost-mechanic family, scoped.
3. **Round-61: warp_cost** — only 3 single-gated / 15,436 decks but a
   known scoped §2 cost-mechanic; clears the warp gate so the 7
   multi-gated warp cards reduce to their *other* gate (turns
   multi-gated into single-gated downstream — a leverage multiplier
   for later rounds).
4. Continue the **Effect-uncovered** per-card substrate lane in
   parallel (it is the campaign's existing primary lane; the 31
   single-gated members are the ranked worklist) and let **N1 own
   BECOMES_TAPPED** via the Seedship Agrarian residue card.
5. Defer **Station / Spacecraft**, **Rooms/doors**, **planeswalker**,
   **carrier-activated**, **static/layered** — all sprawling with low
   or zero single-gate yield; revisit once the medium unlocks have
   collapsed the multi-gate residue.
