### Sub-shape A — Spell-local `CostReduction` rider (2 cards, 24,908 decks) — EoE

| Card | Parsed structure | 17lands decks | Oracle rider |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Gigastorm Titan | `CostReduction(amount=3, cond=AnotherSpellCastThisTurn)` | 14,844 | "This spell costs {3} less to cast if you've cast another spell this turn." |
| Lashwhip Predator | `CostReduction(amount=2, cond=OpponentsControlAtLeast)` | 10,064 | "This spell costs {2} less to cast if your opponents control three or more creatures." + Reach |

Parser view: the rider IS structured into `printing.cost_reductions`
(a tuple of `CostReduction(amount, condition)`). Python's
`apply_cost_reductions` (`actions.py:406`) is called on the **cast
pipeline** (`actions.py:1583`) every time the spell is cast and, when
the structural `Condition` matches, returns a reduced `ManaCost`.

less generic mana when its condition holds. **Rust model:** Rust's
`CardPrinting` struct (`rust/src/cards.rs:4168`) has **no
`cost_reductions` field**; `rust/src/cards.rs:4123` *explicitly defers*
it ("The richer Python fields — … `cost_reductions` … — are deferred
to follow-up slices that bring their gates along"). Rust pays the full
printed `mana_cost`. **Bridge round-trips IDENTICALLY today?** **NO —
and it is not even bridgeable today: `CardDef`
(`cross_engine.py:106`) has no cost-reduction field, and
`build_rust_state`'s `printing_dict` (`cross_engine.py:8483`) emits no
cost-reduction key.** If admitted, Python casts the creature for the
reduced cost, Rust casts it for the full cost → divergent mana pool /
floating-mana state the moment the card is cast.

## 2. Bridge-correctness audit (CRITICAL — honest verdict per sub-shape)

The §2 discipline (HANDOFF §2, round-42 I2): **only admit a shape both
engines model identically; decline silent-mistranslation paths; do NOT
recommend admitting a shape just because it raises the count.**

The dominant structural fact discovered in the verify pass:

> **Cost-reduction is carried by NEITHER engine through the bridge.**
> - `CardDef` (`cross_engine.py:106`) has **no** cost-reduction field
>   (the dataclass enumerates only the "fields both engines understand
>   from a parity standpoint" — cost-reduction is not among them).
> - `build_rust_state`'s `printing_dict` (`cross_engine.py:8483-8530`)
>   emits **no** cost-reduction key — there is nothing to round-trip.
> - Rust's `CardPrinting` struct (`rust/src/cards.rs:4168`) has **no**
>   `cost_reductions` / `external_cost_reductions` field; the ONLY
>   mention in all of `rust/src/` is the comment at
>   `rust/src/cards.rs:4123` *explicitly deferring* it. Rust has no
>   `apply_cost_reductions` analogue anywhere — it pays the full
>   printed `mana_cost`.
> - Python, by contrast, *does* model it fully: `apply_cost_reductions`
>   (`actions.py:406`) is invoked **unconditionally on the cast
>   pipeline** (`actions.py:1583`, and again on the `legal_actions`
>   affordability probe) and reduces the `ManaCost` whenever the
>   structural `Condition` matches.

### The hard distinction from the round-39 Ward / round-59 sub-shape-A precedents

Round-59 admitted the 5 pay-life shocklands as a *documented deliberate
simplification* because the dropped rider was bit-equal under the
bench's strategy: the parser dropped pay-life on **both** bridge sides
(symmetric drop → still bit-equal), AND the `attack_all` strategy never
makes the pay-life decision. Round-39 Ward was the same: Ward only
fires when a spell *targets* the warded creature, and `attack_all`
never targets it.

**Cost-reduction does NOT have that property.** The drop is
**asymmetric** (Python applies the reduction, Rust does not — there is
no symmetric-drop bit-equality) **and** it fires on a path the bench
strategy *always* exercises:

- **Sub-shapes A & D** (spell-local): the reduction is applied the
  moment **the gated creature itself is cast** — and the bench plays
  creatures to attack with them, so it WILL cast them. Python pays the
  reduced cost; Rust pays the full cost. The post-cast mana-pool /
  floating-mana / available-mana state diverges on the **first cast of
  the card** → the bench's state fingerprint mismatches immediately.
  This is precisely the `BECOMES_TAPPED` / `dynamic_pt_formula` class
  of §2 decline: a field one engine models and the bridge cannot carry
  to the other, on a path the harness drives.
- **Sub-shapes B & C** (external static): *worse* — once the permanent
  is on the battlefield, the divergence is not confined to casting that
  card; it fires on **every** qualifying spell the controller casts
  (Uthros: every 2nd spell each turn; Sami: every spell, scaled by
  artifact count). Admitting these would inject a divergence into the
  entire downstream cast sequence of any game where the card resolves.

There is no `attack_all`-conditional "the strategy never triggers it"
escape hatch here, and no symmetric-drop bit-equality. Admitting any of
the 5 by widening the filter gate would ship a **guaranteed
inter-engine divergence**, not a documented simplification.

### §2 classes

#### Class 1 — ADMIT-CLEAN (bridge-correct, propose now)

**None.** Zero of the 5 is bridge-correct to admit in any near-term

#### Class 2 — DECLINE: silent (here, *guaranteed*) inter-engine divergence

**All 5 (sub-shapes A, B, C, D).** Every one would produce a real
cross-engine divergence the moment the card is cast (A/D) or the moment
it is on the battlefield and a qualifying spell is cast (B/C). Decline
until a cost-reduction substrate lands on **both** the CardDef bridge
**AND** Rust (`apply_cost_reductions` + the `Condition` grammar) — a
genuine multi-round substrate, see §3.

#### Class 3 — DECLINE: needs a prior substrate

The same 5, viewed by what the substrate must build. The blocker is not
"one missing field" — it is an entire **cost-reduction subsystem**:
1. A CardDef cost-reduction representation (the
   `(amount, condition)` tuples for `cost_reductions` and
   `external_cost_reductions`).
2. A Rust `CardPrinting` cost-reduction field + a Rust
   `apply_cost_reductions` analogue invoked on the Rust cast pipeline
   + a Rust battlefield scan for externals.
3. A **port of the structural `Condition` grammar** to Rust — the
   reductions are gated by `AnotherSpellCastThisTurn`,
   `OpponentsControlAtLeast`, `NthSpellThisTurn(2)`,
   `PerControlled(IsArtifact)`, `PerDifferentlyNamedLand`. Each is a
   stateful per-turn / per-battlefield predicate Rust does not model.
   This is the largest piece — it is the campaign-wide §2 reason
   `static_abilities` and the cost-mechanic family (warp/Ward/kicker)
   are all declined: conditional/layered/stateful evaluation.
4. Bridge arms in both directions to round-trip the new representation.

This is the same family as `warp_cost` (round-30 Mechanozoa precedent —
a §2-declined cost-mechanic), `ward_cost` (round-39), and
`kicker`/`impending` (roadmap). Cost-reduction is *strictly larger*
than warp/Ward because it is not a single optional fixed cost — it is a
**conditional, variable, stateful** modifier of every cast, requiring a
`Condition`-grammar port, not just a `ManaCost` field.

## 3. Phased implementation plan (this IS a multi-round substrate)

There is **no Python-bridge-only phase-1** for cost-reduction (contrast
non-basic-land's sub-shape A). Every phase below requires a Rust change
and a `maturin develop --release` rebuild; the substrate is genuinely
multi-round. Each phase must leave the gate green
(`tests/test_parity_smoke.py + test_cross_engine_etb_triggers.py +
test_trajectory_parity.py + test_trajectory.py`).

### Phase 1 (Rust + bridge) — CardDef cost-reduction representation + Rust field + the boolean-condition subset

**Scope:** sub-shape A's two cards **only** (Gigastorm Titan, Lashwhip
Predator) — the spell-local boolean-condition shape, the smallest
self-contained slice. Requires:
1. `CardDef` gains a `cost_reductions: tuple[...]` representation
   (the `(amount, condition_kind, condition_args)` triple — frozen,
   bridge-serializable).
2. `build_rust_state`'s `printing_dict` emits the cost-reduction key;
   Rust's `register_printing` parses it into a new `CardPrinting`
   field.
3. Rust gains an `apply_cost_reductions` analogue invoked on the Rust
   cast pipeline, restricted in this phase to the two boolean
   conditions sub-shape A uses (`AnotherSpellCastThisTurn`,
   `OpponentsControlAtLeast`) — each needs a Rust port of the
   per-turn/battlefield predicate.
4. Bidirectional bridge arms in `_card_def_to_python_printing` /
   `build_rust_state` to round-trip the representation.
5. Widen the filter at `trajectory.py:3313` to admit a printing whose
   `cost_reductions` use ONLY the ported boolean conditions and whose
   `external_cost_reductions` is empty (a positive allowlist, the §2
   guardrail — like round-59's `_printing_is_bridge_nonbasic_mana_land`
   but on condition-kind).
- **Pin plan:** Rust regression pins for the new field + the two
  conditions; cross-engine bridge pins that a Gigastorm Titan / Lashwhip
  Predator deck casts the creature for the SAME reduced cost on both
  engines (the divergence this closes); a negative pin that sub-shapes
  B/C/D stay filtered.
- **Genuine-real-EoE-probe:** a mirror game running a real 17lands
  Gigastorm Titan archetype through `attack_all`, asserting 0
  divergence through the turn the Titan is cast (the
  cast-cost-reduction is the exact path that diverges today).
- **Phase-1 EoE-pool delta (only if shipped):** +2 cards / +24,908
  decks. **This is a Rust-agent slice, not a docs/Python-only one — it
  cannot be a round-60 phase-1 the way non-basic-land sub-shape A was.**
