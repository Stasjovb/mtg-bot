# Effect-uncovered Tier-C — the "closest-to-A" subgroup triage

## 0. Headline finding — this is a ONE-substrate cohort, not 28 per-card asks

> **The 28 "closest-to-A" cards (94,339 17lands decks) are gated by
> EXACTLY `{effect_uncovered, nonsafe_triggered_ability}` and nothing
> else** (verified live: the exact-set filter over
> `scout_r63_effect_uncovered_triage.subsystem_attribution()` yields
> precisely 28 cards / 94,339 decks; the deck total is unchanged
> whether or not the 11 zero-deck off-set members are counted —
> **17 cards carry all 94,339 EoE decks; 11 are zero-deck off-set
> card-count-only**). They are NOT gated by any *hard* subsystem
> (Station / Aura / warp / activated-ability / Rooms / planeswalker /
> X-cost). The `nonsafe_triggered_ability` gate is the SOLE blocker
> beyond the bespoke Effect.

The load-bearing consequence (stated up front so the scheduled routines
do not re-derive it):

> **Reading all 28 cards' live `resolve` bodies, the bespoke Effect
> half of nearly every one is itself a clean, deterministic, Tier-A-
> shaped close** (a flag flip, a counter add, a life-set, a draw, a
> targeted bounce/destroy, a token create). The card stays Tier-C ONLY
> because that Effect is **wired to a triggered ability whose
> `(event, predicate, effect_kind)` triple is outside the
> `_SAFE_TRIGGERS` allowlist** (`trajectory.py:1491`). So this is NOT
> 28 independent Effect asks — it is **one substrate lane (the
> `_SAFE_TRIGGERS` allowlist-extension lever, HANDOFF §3 workstream 2)
> intersected with the per-card Effect cadence already running for
> Tier-A.** Closing a card here is the **two-lever close** the triage
> §5 named: (a) bespoke Rust `Effect` + bridge arm + genuine probe
> (the SAME per-card slice shape as Tier-A — drains the
> Effect-uncovered pool-parity bucket), AND (b) a `_SAFE_TRIGGERS`
> allowlist entry for that card's trigger triple (flips the
> production-deck bench *admit*, generating real trajectory signal).
> Lever (a) is per-card; lever (b) is **shared by trigger-family** —
> one safe-trigger triple, once admitted, flips EVERY card whose
> Effect rides that triple. **That is the unlock ladder's structure:
> sequence by trigger-family, not by card.**

This is the in-miniature shape of the spell-card-bridge lever
(`docs/SPELL_CARD_BRIDGE_LEVER_DESIGN.md`) and the Station-subsystem
analogue — one substrate, many consumers — except the substrate here is
the *already-built* `_SAFE_TRIGGERS` mechanism extended one triple at a
time, not a from-scratch loader/CardDef rewrite. It is correspondingly
cheaper per rung.

## 1. Why exactly Tier-C, not Tier-A — the live-resolve evidence

`docs/EFFECT_UNCOVERED_TRIAGE.md` §4 is the single-gated
(`effect_uncovered` ALONE) Tier-A lane: those cards are Instants/
Sorceries whose `spell_effect` IS the whole card — closing the Effect
flips the pool-parity card. The 28 cards here differ on **one axis
only**: the missing Effect is the body of a **triggered ability on a
permanent** (a creature/artifact/enchantment/land), not a spell's
`spell_effect`. The card therefore co-trips `nonsafe_triggered_ability`
— the bench loader's `_filter_deck_to_bridge_supported` admits a
creature only if every triggered ability it carries is on the
`_SAFE_TRIGGERS` allowlist (`trajectory.py:2462-2466`), and these
triples are not (yet) on it.

`_SAFE_TRIGGERS` today (verified live `trajectory.py:1491-1593`):

```
("enters", "source_is_self", "draw_cards")
("enters", "source_is_self", "gain_life")
("enters", "source_is_self", "scry_n")
("enters", "source_is_self", "surveil")
("dies",   "source_is_self", "surveil")
("attacks","source_is_self", "surveil")
… (+ the landfall ENTERS+land extension)
```

Extending it is a **lockstep operation** (verified verbatim,
`trajectory.py:1483-1490`): each new triple needs (a) the matching
cross-engine bridge arm to ALREADY exist on both engines, and (b) a
trajectory probe demonstrating end-to-end firing parity. The bespoke
Effect close (lever a) IS what makes (a) true for that triple. So the
two levers are **causally ordered**: ship the Effect + bridge arm
first (Tier-A-shaped slice), THEN the `_SAFE_TRIGGERS` triple becomes
admissible (a small follow-up extension slice + its parity probe).

## 2. The dependency-ordered UNLOCK LADDER (substrate → cards → deck payoff)

The ladder is keyed by **trigger-family substrate rung** because lever
(b) — the `_SAFE_TRIGGERS` triple — is shared by every card whose
Effect rides that trigger family. Each rung's prerequisite is the
per-card lever (a) work (the bespoke Effect + bridge arm, the same
Tier-A cadence) for the cards on that rung; the rung itself is then a
single small `_SAFE_TRIGGERS` extension slice (one triple + its
end-to-end firing-parity probe, per the lockstep contract
`trajectory.py:1483-1490`). Rungs are ordered by **EoE-deck payoff per
shared-substrate slice** (the continuous-pipeline leverage criterion),
hard-substrate-free rungs first.

> **Sequencing prerequisite for the WHOLE ladder.** This entire lane is
> the HANDOFF §3 *successor* to the single-round Tier-A queue. Per
> `docs/EFFECT_UNCOVERED_TIERA_MINISPECS_BATCH3.md` §0/§4 and the
> triage §7 it is explicitly **NOT** to be started until the round-64
> (#1-7) + batch-2 (B1-B6) + batch-3 (B7-B8) Tier-A queue drains AND
> the `docs/SPELL_CARD_BRIDGE_LEVER_DESIGN.md` shared lever lands
> (those are higher-leverage). This doc scopes the lane so it is
> implement-ready *when* the orchestrator reaches it — it does not
> reprioritise it ahead of those.
